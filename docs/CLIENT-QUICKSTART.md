# Executive Desk: quick start for users

**Your first five minutes**
1. Open Setup. Enter your name or company, add your logo, pick a colour.
2. Add today's meetings (Meetings, Add meeting), or connect your calendar.
3. On Today, type your three priorities for the day.
4. In Delegation, add anything you are waiting on. Use Copy nudge to send a follow-up.

**Connect your calendar**
- Quick way: in your calendar app, export your calendar as a .ics file. In Setup, choose Choose .ics files.
- Automatic way: ask your administrator to set up live calendar feeds.

**Protect your information**
- Everything is stored in this browser on this device only.
- Use Setup, Export backup regularly and keep the text somewhere safe.
- If you use another device or browser, restore from your backup there.
- Treat calendar links like passwords.

**Weekly habit:** open Review on Friday, check what is open, and print it if you like.
