# Owner notes: how to sell Executive Desk (kept out of the repo by .gitignore)

No sale is guaranteed. These are suggestions to test, not promises.

## Before you sell (checklist)
1. Test live feeds with your own Google and Outlook calendars on your own hosted copy and relay. Note how late changes appear.
2. Test printing and backup download on the hosted copy.
3. Have a lawyer review LICENSE.txt, especially the no-refunds clause under South African consumer and e-commerce law (online buyers may have cooling-off rights that cannot be removed), and write each customer's name into their copy.
4. Pick a payment platform that can pay you out in your country (Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, Gumroad, or a direct invoice).
5. Refund and support terms are set in the licence (no refunds, support by email). Make sure your listing says the same.
6. Keep this repository PRIVATE. A public repo lets anyone copy the product for free. If you want a public page, publish only the README and screenshots in a separate repo.

## Positioning
"A private daily desk for executives. One file, no accounts, your data stays on your device, with your branding."
Buyers: executives, chiefs of staff, executive assistants, small agencies that serve executives.

## Offer ideas (prices are starting points to test)
- Licence only (self-host, one organisation): roughly $150 to $400.
- Done for you (branding, hosting, relay and calendar connection, 30-minute handover): roughly $800 to $2,500.
- Care plan (monthly check, updates, support): roughly $30 to $100 a month.
A single $4,000 sale most realistically comes from a done-for-you setup for one executive or a small agency, not from the licence alone.

## 60-second demo script
1. Today screen: "Here is the whole day, and what needs attention."
2. Open a meeting, show the prep list, turn action items into tasks.
3. Delegation: Copy nudge.
4. Setup: change the logo and colour to the client's brand, live.
5. Close: "Your data never leaves your device. I can set it up for you this week."

## Common questions
- Is my data safe? It stays in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to me. Back up regularly.
- Does it sync my calendar live? It refreshes a private calendar link automatically, but Google and Microsoft decide how fast their links update, so changes can be hours late.
- Does it use AI? No. It is a planner.
- What if I clear my browser? Restore from your backup. We set up a backup habit on day one.
